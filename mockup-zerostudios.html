I. Early Life in the Ashen Wastes

[ERA // 842 2A]

O f the early years of this person little is set down in the high annals, and what remains is fraught with conflicting oral testimonies from clan elders. Born during the bitter winter of the Second Siege of Valoria, she was fostered among the torch-makers of the Western Bastion, trained in both ceremonial lantern craft and the defensive spear forms of the Ashen Guard.

Before taking the solemn oath at the Obsidian Gateway, she traveled with the nomadic iron caravans across the Varkas Pass , where she first discovered the shattered fragments of the Void Crucible. It was here that she received the epithet “The Lamplighter” after guiding forty survivors through the blind blizzard of 856 2A.

“When the sun hides behind the iron peaks, it is not light we pray for, but the courage to bear the torch through the silence.”

— Journal of the High Scribe, Codex IV · Stanza 12

In later chronicles, her refusal to kneel before King Aldric’s envoy led directly to the Schism of High Keep. While loyalists marked her as an apostate, the common clans of the low valleys preserve her name as a guardian spirit who tends the boundary fires.
